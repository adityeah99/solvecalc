Simplifying a fraction means writing it with smaller numbers without changing its value. For example, 4/8 and 1/2 are the same amount. If you cut a cake into 8 slices and take 4, you have half the cake.

A fraction is in its **simplest form** when the top and bottom numbers have no common factor except 1. That means there is no whole number bigger than 1 that divides into both of them.

## The main idea

To simplify, you divide the numerator (top) and the denominator (bottom) by the same number. Doing the same thing to both keeps the value the same.

The fastest way is to divide by the **greatest common divisor** (GCD). This is the biggest number that divides into both. Some books call it the highest common factor, or HCF. It is the same thing.

## How to find the GCD

One simple way is to list the factors of each number and find the biggest one they share.

For 18 and 24:

- Factors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18
- Factors of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24
- Factors they share: 1, 2, 3, 6
- The biggest shared factor is 6, so the GCD is 6.

## Example: simplify 18/24

1. Find the GCD of 18 and 24. From the lists above, it is 6.
2. Divide the top by 6: 18 ÷ 6 = 3
3. Divide the bottom by 6: 24 ÷ 6 = 4
4. The answer is 3/4.

Can 3/4 be simplified any more? The only number that divides into both 3 and 4 is 1, so no. It is in simplest form.

## Example: simplify 45/75

Say you answered 45 out of 75 questions correctly across a term of homework. As a fraction, that is 45/75.

1. Factors of 45: 1, 3, 5, 9, 15, 45
2. Factors of 75: 1, 3, 5, 15, 25, 75
3. The biggest shared factor is 15.
4. Divide both by 15: 45 ÷ 15 = 3 and 75 ÷ 15 = 5
5. The answer is 3/5.

So you got 3 out of every 5 questions right.

## Another way: divide in small steps

If you cannot spot the GCD straight away, that is fine. Keep dividing by small numbers that go into both, like 2, 3 or 5, until you cannot go any further.

### Example: simplify 48/72

1. Both are even, so divide by 2: 24/36
2. Still both even, divide by 2 again: 12/18
3. Still both even, divide by 2 again: 6/9
4. Both divide by 3: 2/3
5. 2 and 3 share no factor except 1, so stop. The answer is 2/3.

This took four steps. If you had known the GCD of 48 and 72 is 24, you could have done it in one: 48 ÷ 24 = 2 and 72 ÷ 24 = 3. Both ways give the same answer.

## Quick tests for common factors

These little checks help you spot a number to divide by:

- **2**: both numbers are even.
- **3**: the digits of each number add up to a multiple of 3. For 45, 4 + 5 = 9, so 3 divides 45.
- **5**: both numbers end in 0 or 5.
- **10**: both numbers end in 0.

## Simplifying fractions bigger than 1

The same method works when the top is bigger than the bottom. Take 20/8:

1. The GCD of 20 and 8 is 4.
2. 20 ÷ 4 = 5 and 8 ÷ 4 = 2, so 20/8 = 5/2
3. As a mixed number, 5/2 = 2 1/2.

## Mistakes to watch for

- **Dividing only one number.** If you divide the top by 6 but not the bottom, you change the value of the fraction.
- **Subtracting instead of dividing.** Taking 2 away from the top and bottom of 4/6 gives 2/4, which is a different amount. Always divide.
- **Stopping too early.** 12/18 is simpler than 48/72, but it is not the simplest form. Keep going until the only common factor is 1.
- **Trying to simplify across a plus sign.** You simplify a single fraction, not parts of a sum. Add first, then simplify.

## Try it yourself

1. Simplify 30/42.
2. Simplify 16/40.

**Answers:** 1) The GCD is 6, so 30/42 = 5/7. 2) The GCD is 8, so 16/40 = 2/5.
