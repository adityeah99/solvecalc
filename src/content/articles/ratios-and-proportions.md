A ratio compares two amounts, showing how much of one there is next to the other. A proportion says that two ratios are equal, which lets you scale things up or down.

If a recipe uses 2 cups of flour to 3 cups of milk, the ratio is 2 to 3, written 2:3. Ratios are everywhere: mixing paint, reading maps, and sharing things fairly.

## Writing and reading a ratio

A ratio can be written with a colon (2:3), in words (2 to 3), or as a fraction (2/3). The order matters: 2:3 is not the same as 3:2.

The numbers in a ratio are called its terms. Keep them in the order the question gives you, so flour-to-milk stays flour first.

## Simplifying a ratio

Simplify a ratio the same way you simplify a fraction: divide both terms by their greatest common factor.

### Worked example: simplify 12:18

1. Find the greatest common factor of 12 and 18. It is 6.
2. Divide both terms by 6: 12 ÷ 6 = 2 and 18 ÷ 6 = 3.

So 12:18 simplifies to **2:3**. This is the same comparison written in the smallest whole numbers.

## Solving a proportion

A proportion is two equal ratios, such as 3/4 = x/20. To find the missing value, use cross-multiplication: multiply each top by the opposite bottom and set them equal.

### Worked example: solve 3/4 = x/20

1. Cross-multiply: 3 × 20 = 4 × x, which is 60 = 4x.
2. Divide both sides by 4: x = 60 ÷ 4 = 15.

So x = **15**. Check: 15/20 simplifies to 3/4, so the ratios match.

## A real-world proportion

A recipe uses flour and milk in the ratio 2:3. If you use 9 cups of milk, how much flour do you need?

1. Set up the proportion: 2/3 = x/9 (flour over milk).
2. Cross-multiply: 2 × 9 = 3 × x, so 18 = 3x.
3. Divide by 3: x = 18 ÷ 3 = 6.

You need **6 cups of flour**. Check: 6:9 simplifies to 2:3, the original ratio.

## Common mistakes

- **Swapping the order.** A ratio of 2:3 is different from 3:2. Keep the terms in the order the problem states.
- **Simplifying only one term.** You must divide both parts by the same number. 12:18 does not become 2:18.
- **Cross-multiplying the wrong pairs.** Multiply each numerator by the opposite denominator, not a top by its own bottom.
- **Forgetting the units.** If the ratio compares cups to cups, your answer is in cups too.

## Try it yourself

1. Simplify the ratio 20:35.
2. Solve the proportion 2/5 = x/20.

**Answers:** 1) The greatest common factor of 20 and 35 is 5, so 20 ÷ 5 = 4 and 35 ÷ 5 = 7, giving **4:7**. 2) Cross-multiply: 2 × 20 = 5 × x, so 40 = 5x and x = **8**.
